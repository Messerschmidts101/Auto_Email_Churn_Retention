Section II - Writing Churn Email Template
===============================

Guidelines for effective retention emails:

1. **Tone & Voice**
   - Personal, empathetic, and proactive
   - Reinforce value and address specific concerns
   - Avoid sounding generic or automated

2. **Structure**
   - Greeting with name
   - Highlight issue/reason subtly
   - Offer resolution or incentive
   - Strong CTA (Call to Action)
   - Closing that invites dialogue

3. Redeem and Voucher Code Format: {{Customer Name}}_D&W_{{Segment Number}}

4. **Template Example**

Subject: Let's Stay Connected, {{Customer Name}}

Hi {{Customer Name}},

{{Intro about recognizing reason for churning and being empathetic to their problems.}}

{{Body about what is our company all about, and why they are important to us.}}

{{Retention offer details. Do not mention what segment they are.}}

Warmest regards,
Das & Will Telco Team!

Section III - Retention Offer Strategy
===============================
Segment 1: New, Low Engagement
- Trigger: Tenure < 1 year, IsActiveMember = 0
- Offer: Free 10GB data for 3 months

Segment 2: Financially Stable, Low Balance
- Trigger: CreditScore > 700, Balance < 100
- Offer: 10% discount voucher, locked-in rates for 12 months

Segment 3: Senior Loyalists
- Trigger: Age > 50, Tenure > 5 years
- Offer: 12% discount voucher, priority support line

Segment 4: Dissatisfied Customers
- Trigger: RecentSatisfactionScore < 3
- Offer: Direct callback, priority support line, $10 Amazon Gift Card
- **CTA:** Encourage user to fill out feedback on the survey page.  

Segment 5: Credit Card Inactive Users
- Trigger: HasCrCard = 0, IsActiveMember = 1
- Offer: 30% discount on tier upgrade
